Dust and Starlight

Did the Bible get there first?

Each episode sets one ancient line of Scripture beside one modern scientific discovery and asks whether they agree. Sometimes science lands right where the text said it would. Sometimes it has to take back what it taught. Told from a creation point of view, and upfront about where the case is strong and where it's still debated. For believers, willing skeptics, and anyone who loves a "wait, really?" moment.

Voices are AI-generated, and guests are fictional characters.
