Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy covers the Dust and Starlight website and the "Dust and Starlight Publisher" app, a private tool the show's creator uses to publish the show's own videos to its own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and collects no personal information. The Publisher app is used only with the Dust and Starlight accounts owned by the show's creator. It does not collect, store or share any data about other users or viewers.

Platform permissions

When the show's creator connects the Publisher app to a platform, the platform issues an access token that allows uploading the show's videos and reading basic information about the show's own account. These tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in the operating system's encrypted keychain. They are never sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Third-party platforms

Videos published by the show appear on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and Spotify, which have their own privacy policies.
